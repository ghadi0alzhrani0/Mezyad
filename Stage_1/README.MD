# Portfolio Project - Team Formation, Research, Brainstorming and MVP (Stage 1)

## Overview & Purpose
This document outlines the requirements, guidelines, and structural framework for Stage 1 of the portfolio project. The primary purpose of this stage is to establish a cohesive, cross-functional team, explore complex real-world engineering problems through structured brainstorming, evaluate project feasibility from both technical and commercial perspectives, and formally define the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) concept.

---

## Objectives of Stage 1
* Build a cohesive, highly communicative, and effective cross-functional team with clear collaboration dynamics and shared accountability.
* Brainstorm and explore multiple high-level project ideas while fostering engineering creativity and innovation.
* Analyze and rigorously evaluate the technical feasibility, testability, and problem-solving impact of different engineering concepts.
* Select and define the final MVP concept (High-Value Assets & Investment Opportunities Auction Platform) that aligns with team goals and modern web standards.
* Document the complete decision-making process, including gap analysis, framework applications, and scope boundaries.

---

## Tasks to Complete Stage 1

### 0. Team Formation & Roles Definition
* **Initial Alignment:** Hold initial team meetings to introduce members, align backgrounds, and establish working norms.
* **Cross-Functional Model:** Assign all team members as Full-Stack Developers to ensure collective ownership of database schemas, frontend interfaces, backend servers, and real-time WebSockets.
* **Communication Governance:** Establish strict on-site core hours (Daily from 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM at the academy headquarters) for collaborative pair programming and peer code reviews, supplemented by Discord for async communication and Notion as the central command center.

### 1. Research and Brainstorming
* **Market Gap Analysis:** Conduct deep research on local and global marketplaces (e.g., traditional classifieds vs. specialized luxury auction houses like Sotheby's and Bring a Trailer) to identify critical pain points such as fake bids, update latency, and lack of secure liquidation channels.
* **SCAMPER Framework:** Apply Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, and Eliminate techniques to innovate the traditional auction lifecycle.
* **"How Might We" (HMW) Questions:** Formulate open-ended architectural prompts to address sub-second real-time latency, race conditions during concurrency, and programmatic financial escrow locks.
* **Project Concept Generation:** Brainstorm and document four advanced engineering concepts:
  1. Aegis-Grid (Dynamic Routing & Distributed Resource Management)
  2. Titan-Protocol (Sensitive Facilities Management & Security Control)
  3. SentinX (Tactical Operations Intelligence & Management System)
  4. High-Value Assets Auction Platform (Selected MVP Concept)

### 2. Idea Evaluation & Comparative Analysis
* **Evaluation Criteria:** Define strict metrics covering technical feasibility for a Web MVP, data testability, commercial viability, and real-world problem-solving capacity.
* **Comparative Matrix:** Score and rank the brainstormed concepts to objectively justify the final selection and document explicit reasons for rejecting complex simulation-dependent ideas.

### 3. Decision and Refinement (MVP Definition)
* **Final Selection:** Unanimously select the High-Value Assets Auction Platform.
* **Concept Refinement:** Define the core problem, proposed solution, target audience, and user roles (Bidder, Seller, Admin).
* **Scope Boundaries:** Delineate strict In-Scope features (Authentication, Escrow simulation, Real-time WebSocket Bidding Room, Anti-Sniper Engine, Admin Approval Workflow) from Out-of-Scope items (Heavy frontend frameworks like React/Next.js, live banking APIs, AI/ML, and Blockchain).

### 4. Idea Development Documentation
* Formalize all research, brainstorming sessions, evaluation matrices, and architectural decisions into a comprehensive Stage 1 Report (`Report.md`) located within the stage directory.
